# CtrlCVCV Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 13, 2026

CtrlCVCV ("the extension") is a Chrome side panel that stores answers to job application questions and copies them to your clipboard. This policy explains what information the extension handles and what happens to it.

**In short:** everything you enter stays on your computer. The extension has no servers, sends nothing over the internet, and doesn't track you.

## Information you enter

You choose what to store in the extension. This can include:

- Links, such as your LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio addresses
- Your phone number
- Your desired salary
- Dates, such as a start date or graduation date
- Cover letters and other written answers
- Short answers, such as your notice period, work authorization or pronouns
- Job details for the application you're working on: the company name, position and hiring manager's name
- Your settings, such as section names, theme and display preferences

## Where it is stored

Everything is saved in your browser with Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on the device you're using. It is:

- **Not** sent to the developer or anyone else
- **Not** synced to your Google account or other devices
- **Not** stored on any server. The extension has no servers.

If you have the side panel open in more than one Chrome window, those panels share the same local data.

## Information the extension does not collect

The extension does **not**:

- Make any network requests or contact any server
- Use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind
- Read web pages, form fields or your browsing history. It can't see the sites you visit.
- Read your clipboard. It only writes to it when you click to copy.
- Use cookies
- Sell, rent or share your information

## Permissions

The extension asks Chrome for only three permissions:

| Permission | Why it's needed |
| --- | --- |
| `sidePanel` | Shows the extension in Chrome's side panel when you click its toolbar icon. |
| `storage` | Saves the answers and settings you enter on your device. |
| `clipboardWrite` | Copies a value to your clipboard when you click it, so you can paste it into an application form. |

It doesn't request host permissions, content scripts or access to your tabs, so it can't read or change web pages.

## Actions you start

Some features only run when you click them:

- **Copy:** puts the value you clicked on your clipboard. What happens after you paste it is up to you and the site you paste into.
- **Open link:** opens a link you stored (for example, your portfolio) in a new tab. The site you open has its own privacy policy.
- **Export backup:** downloads a JSON file of your data to your computer. That file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. The extension never uploads it.
- **Import backup:** reads a backup file you choose and replaces your saved data with it. The file isn't sent anywhere.

## Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays until you change or delete it. You can:

- Edit or delete any field or section on the **Edit** page
- Clear the job details with the **Clear** button on the **Use** page
- Choose **Reset to defaults** in the ⋮ menu to erase everything you entered
- Remove the extension from Chrome, which deletes all of its stored data

Because nothing is stored anywhere else, the developer can't access, recover or delete your data for you.

## Security

Your data is only as secure as your computer and Chrome profile. Anyone who can use your Chrome profile can open the side panel and see what you saved. Don't store passwords, government ID numbers or bank details in the extension; it isn't designed for them.

## Children

The extension is meant for people applying for jobs and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, because it doesn't collect information at all.

## Chrome Web Store user data policy

The extension's use of information follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including its Limited Use requirements. Information you enter is used only to provide the extension's features to you, and is never transferred, sold or used for any other purpose.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the extension handles information, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Any change that would send data off your device would be described here before it takes effect.
